Privacy Notice

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This notice explains, in plain terms, how this app handles your information.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect the information needed to set up and run that account, such as your email address and the sign-in details provided by your chosen sign-in method (for example, Google Sign-In). We also store the watchlist data you build inside the app: the movies and TV shows you add, your watch progress, and your preferences, so it's there the next time you sign in. This includes a streaming region setting, either detected automatically from your device or chosen by you, which is used only to show accurate "where to watch" information for your country.

What we use it for

We use your information for one purpose only: to create and operate your account and to provide the app's features to you (such as syncing your library across devices and sending release alerts you've opted into). We do not use your information for anything else: no advertising, no selling or sharing it with third parties for marketing, and no profiling beyond what's needed to run the app itself.

Third-party services

This app retrieves publicly available movie and TV metadata from The Movie Database (TMDB) to display titles, posters, and related information, and streaming-availability information from JustWatch to show where a title can be watched. Both are one-way lookups of show/movie data; neither is linked to your personal account information.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated library data at any time from within the app, under your profile on the Home tab. No need to contact us. If you'd rather we do it for you, or you're unable to access the app, email delete@novawatch.site and we'll take care of it.

Questions

If you have any questions about this notice or how your information is handled, email info@novawatch.site.

Credits & attribution

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Movie and TV metadata, posters, and artwork are sourced from The Movie Database (TMDB). Streaming availability information is provided by JustWatch.
